From conversations to reusable knowledge
A practical foundation for communities where discussions should stay searchable and useful.

Run public forums, Q&A communities, product spaces, and internal knowledge hubs.

Keep discussions, accepted answers, articles, moderation, search, and member operations in one system.

Why BBS-GO

Built for communities that need lasting knowledge

A lighter self-hosted alternative when chat is too noisy, docs are not interactive enough, and enterprise forum suites feel too heavy.

Forum, Q&A, and articles in one place

Support open-ended discussions, accepted-answer workflows, and knowledge articles without stitching tools together.

Self-hosted and easy to operate

Deploy with Docker or release packages, keep data under your control, and run with a small footprint.

Ready for real moderation

Manage users, roles, topics, comments, settings, and growth mechanics from a focused admin dashboard.

Use Cases

One platform for discussions that should last

Use BBS-GO wherever conversations, answers, and community knowledge need to stay searchable and reusable.

Public and Private Forums
Create topic-driven spaces for products, interests, teams, or vertical communities.
Q&A Communities
Let members ask questions, discuss answers, mark solutions, and build a library of solved issues over time.
Knowledge-Sharing Communities
Publish articles and organize long-lived content alongside community discussion.
Product User Communities
Give users a dedicated place for support, announcements, showcases, and feedback.
Internal Team Spaces
Run a private place for team questions, operational notes, and reusable know-how.
